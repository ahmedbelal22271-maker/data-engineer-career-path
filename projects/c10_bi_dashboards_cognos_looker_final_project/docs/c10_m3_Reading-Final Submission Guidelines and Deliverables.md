# **Reading: Final Submission Guidelines and Deliverables**

**Estimated Time: 2 minutes**

You have completed a series of labs applying key concepts from BI Dashboards with IBM Cognos Analytics and Google Looker Studio through hands-on activities using both tools. Each lab guided you through tasks such as connecting to different data sources, preparing and transforming data, creating meaningful visualizations, applying filters and calculations, and building interactive dashboards and reports to support business insights and decision-making.

The final assignment brings together all the skills you have developed throughout the course. In this exercise, you will take on the role of a data scientist hired by SwiftAuto Traders, a chain of car dealerships. Your task is to analyze car sales and profits for each dealer and present your findings through effective visualizations in the form of a dashboard or report for your regional manager.

You may choose one of the following options to complete the assignment:

Part A: Use IBM Cognos Analytics to create an interactive dashboard.  
OR  
Part B: Use Google Looker Studio to create visuals and dashboards.

## **How can you submit the deliverables**

You can submit your project deliverables in one of the following ways:

### Option 1: AI-Graded Submission and Evaluation

#### Capture and Submit Dashboard Screenshots

1. Create the required dashboards as instructed in the assignment.
2. Take **screenshots of each completed exercise/dashboard**.
3. Save the screenshots in **.png or .jpg format**.
4. Submit the **screenshots** for AI-based evaluation.

Once submitted, you will receive **automated grades instantly**.

### Option 2: Peer-Graded Submission and Evaluation

#### Submit Dashboard Evidence

1. Create the required dashboards as instructed in the assignment.
2. Submit **either a PDF file or screenshots of the dashboard** showing the complete output (as specified).
3. Screenshots should be saved in **.png** or **.jpg** format.
4. Ensure all visuals and results are clearly visible.

We recommend using **Option 1 for faster grading**. However, if you experience difficulties with this method or are unable to access it, you may choose Option 2 instead.

_Note: You are required to choose only one submission option, either AI-Graded or Peer-Graded, and not both, to receive grades for the final assignment._
