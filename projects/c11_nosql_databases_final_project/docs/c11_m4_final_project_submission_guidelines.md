# Final Project Submission Guidelines & Deliverables

**Estimated Study Time:** 13 mins (Deep Comprehension @ 2.5x) | Base Reading: 5 mins

## Overview

The final project evaluates hands-on mastery of MongoDB and Apache Cassandra. Depending on your course workflow, you will complete activities in the Cloud IDE workspace, generate the required terminal outputs and query results, and submit your deliverables through one of two evaluation paths.

---

## Submission Options

You must select **one** submission option (do not submit both):

### Option 1: AI-Graded Submission and Evaluation (Recommended)
- **Workflow:** Complete all required tasks in the Cloud IDE.
- **Evidence Required:**
  1. Capture and save clear screenshots displaying executed commands and full terminal outputs.
  2. Save all executed queries (especially MongoDB aggregation pipelines and CQL statements) in a plain text file.
- **Evaluation:** Upload evidence for immediate automated AI evaluation.

### Option 2: Peer-Graded Submission and Evaluation
- **Workflow:** Complete all 10 project tasks as instructed.
- **Evidence Required:**
  1. Take full-window screenshots of each completed exercise showing the prompt, command, and result output.
  2. Format and save image files in standard `.png` or `.jpg` format.
- **Evaluation:** Graded via peer review rubrics based on uploaded visual evidence.

---

## Important Rules
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Choose only **one** submission track (AI-Graded or Peer-Graded). Submitting across both tracks is prohibited by the course grading policy.
