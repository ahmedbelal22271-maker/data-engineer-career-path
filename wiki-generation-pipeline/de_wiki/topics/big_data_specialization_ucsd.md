## Recurring Case Study: Catch the Pink Flamingo / Eglence Inc.

The specialization uses a fictional multi-user mobile game — **Catch the Pink Flamingo** by **Eglence Inc.** ("eğlence" = Turkish for "fun") — as a recurring case study spanning all 6 courses. The game has millions of players worldwide who capture pink flamingos on a world map, form groups, and compete. Its data ecosystem exhibits all five V's simultaneously and forms the basis of the Course 6 Capstone Project. [Cross-ref: topics/big_data_characteristics_deep_dive.md — Value: Catch the Pink Flamingo case study]

## SDSC and Instructor Context

The specialization is delivered by the **San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC)** at UC San Diego. Ilkay Altintas (Chief Data Science Officer at SDSC) leads the WIFIRE Project (wildfire analytics cyberinfrastructure) and teaches Course 1. Amarnath Gupta (Director of Data Science and lead instructor) guides Courses 1-3 and 5-6. Mai Nguyen (Head of Data Science Education and Outreach at SDSC) teaches Course 4 (Machine Learning With Big Data). The 5 P's of Data Science framework was developed at SDSC.
