# Discovering Your Path to Data Engineering — Interactive Dialogue

Welcome! In this Dialogue, we'll explore how your unique background and career goals connect to the exciting field of data engineering. This will help you personalize your learning journey before we dive into the technical concepts.

## What the Dialogue Covers

1. **Your Starting Point** — Discuss your current background, experience level, and any exposure you've had to data.
2. **Your Why and Where** — Explore what attracted you to data engineering and what you hope to achieve in this field, including potential career paths.
3. **Your Personalized Roadmap** — Collaborate to identify which course topics you want to prioritize based on your goals.
